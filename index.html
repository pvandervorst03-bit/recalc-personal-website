01 / A LITTLE BACKGROUND

Three places.
One through-line.

I grew up in Naperville, Illinois, where Scouting, distance running, orchestra, and a youth business fair taught me that the best teams combine individual preparation with shared responsibility.

At the University of South Carolina, I found finance: a field where numbers matter, but judgment matters more. Since then, I have tried to get as close to the work as possible—from sourcing lower-middle-market businesses to researching technology companies.

A summer in Dordrecht, the Netherlands, made the work more global and more concrete. Building ROIC trees for portfolio companies showed me that “value creation” is really a series of decisions made by real operators.

The thread is simple: enter unfamiliar rooms, listen closely, earn responsibility, and leave something useful behind.
